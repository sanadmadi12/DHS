# About Me

Hi, I'm **Sanad Madi**, an engineering student at **NYU Abu Dhabi**.

This website serves as my digital portfolio for **Data and Human Space**, a course exploring the relationship between data, mapping, technology, and the spaces in which people live and interact.

## About This Site

Throughout the semester, this site will document my work with geospatial data and digital mapping.

The course explores how maps can do more than simply represent geography. Maps can help us investigate human activity, visualize patterns, understand historical and contemporary spaces, and communicate stories through data.

My work on this site will involve tools and approaches such as **OpenStreetMap, QGIS, computational notebooks, open geospatial data, digital cartography, and spatial visualization**.

## My Perspective

Coming from an engineering background, I am particularly interested in the intersection between **technology, data, and physical space**.

I am interested in how computational tools can transform complex spatial datasets into visual representations that make patterns easier to understand and communicate.

This portfolio will develop throughout Fall 2026 as I complete the four major assignments for the course.
