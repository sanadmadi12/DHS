Welcome to Data & Human Space

Welcome to my website for the Core: Data and Human Space course at NYU Abu Dhabi.

Throughout this course, I explore how data, mapping, and digital technologies help us understand human activity and the spaces around us. Through different assignments, I examine how information is collected, processed, and represented, and how the tools and decisions involved influence our understanding of the world.

This website documents my assignments, interactive maps, and reflections throughout the semester.
